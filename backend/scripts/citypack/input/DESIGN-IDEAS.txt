STILL EARLY — ORIGINAL MAP DESIGN DIRECTIONS

RECOMMENDED: WHITE + FOREST GREEN + A LITTLE CORAL
Use a white interface, near-black text, a quiet map and real company logos. Forest green marks selected actions; coral adds a small highlight. Soft green filter chips provide colour without overwhelming the map. Reuse existing approved branding wherever possible.
Suggested fallback tokens: white #FFFFFF; background #F7F8FA; ink #17201D; green #246B50; pale green #EAF2EC; coral #EF7A61.

Desktop composition
A compact header contains the brand, city switcher and Submit a company. Beneath it, a collapsible left panel holds search and a useful list, while the map occupies the remaining space. Selecting either a list row or a logo pin opens the same detail panel. Keep the map page focused on discovery.

Mobile composition
Full-screen map, compact top search/city controls, and a bottom sheet for the list or selected company. Let users switch to an accessible list. Keep zoom controls, provider attribution and touch targets clear of overlays.

OTHER PALETTE OPTIONS
1. White + cobalt + soft peach: a more technical feel, with cobalt reserved for selections and links. Avoid saturated colour across the whole map.
2. Warm white + charcoal + apricot: a warmer community feel, with restrained illustration on the welcome page and a practical map workspace.
Use one token system across welcome, map, company detail and investor pages; don't give every city a different brand.

INTERACTIONS THAT MAKE THE MAP WORTH RETURNING TO
- Fast city switching with Sydney, Melbourne and Perth and preservation of filters.
- Real logos rather than generic dots; readable cluster counts when zoomed out.
- A shared-building picker so multiple companies at one address remain discoverable.
- Saved companies and shareable searches, if the existing account/storage system supports them.
- A clear Map/List toggle so a company can be discovered even without a public street address.
- Short company descriptions, official website links, location status and Suggest a correction.
- A subtle New to Still Early label based on the actual date added, clearly distinct from company founding date.
- Recently checked addresses only when a real address check has occurred.

DATA SHOULD SHAPE THE EXPERIENCE
Use distinct filters for Startups, Companies, Investors and Spaces. Show local office versus local headquarters only when supported. Do not label every investor-backed or listed firm as early stage. Do not show hiring, trending, funding-stage or popularity badges without supporting data.
City-only records must not create a dense pile of misleading pins at a CBD centre. Give them an honest directory experience while awaiting address confirmation.

FUTURE IDEAS AFTER THE MAP WORKS WELL
Small curated lists such as 'Melbourne climate builders' or 'Perth robotics' can link to the same filtered map. Founder-approved profile corrections can improve data quality. Jobs and events can later use separate pages and optional map layers without crowding the core experience.

The inspiration is functional discoverability. The visual layout, wording and implementation should be original to Still Early.
